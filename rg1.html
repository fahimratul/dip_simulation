Region growing across a raster scan

It is not hundred percent accurate

The scan walks the 8×8 image one pixel at a time, left to right and then down to the next row. What happens at each pixel depends on what it is:

  1. Not a seed and not yet in a region — nothing to do, move on.
  2. A seed with no region yet — start a new region here, then test its neighbours.
  3. Already inside a region (seed or not) — test its neighbours and grow that region.

A neighbour is taken in only if it is still free and the region's max − min stays within the threshold after adding it. A pixel turned down by one region can still be picked up by another one later.
