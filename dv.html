📈 1st & 2nd Order Derivative Simulator

Enter a 1-D image strip (a scan line of intensities) and watch the forward 1st derivative and the central 2nd derivative get computed cell by cell, following the standard formulas used in digital image processing.

∂f/∂x = f(x+1) − f(x)
∂²f/∂x² = f(x+1) + f(x−1) − 2·f(x)

Step 1 — Define the Image Strip

Click any cell to edit its value and press Tab to move to the next. The row above the cells shows the position index x (0-based).

Step 2 — Compute the Derivatives

The 1st derivative at position x uses the forward difference f(x+1) − f(x), so position N−1 is undefined. The 2nd derivative uses the central second difference f(x+1) + f(x−1) − 2·f(x), so positions 0 and N−1 are undefined. Undefined cells are shown as dashed boxes.
