Laplacian Filter Simulator

Build a numeric image matrix, pick one of the Laplacian masks (standard or enhanced), zero-pad the border, then step through the convolution cell by cell to see how the second-order spatial derivative highlights intensity discontinuities.

∂²f/∂x² = f(x+1,y) + f(x−1,y) − 2·f(x,y)
∂²f/∂y² = f(x,y+1) + f(x,y−1) − 2·f(x,y)
∇²f = f(x+1,y) + f(x−1,y) + f(x,y+1) + f(x,y−1) − 4·f(x,y)

Step 3 — Zero Pad the Border

Because the mask is 3×3 with the centre as the anchor, a 1-pixel zero-pad on every side is needed so the window has a full neighbourhood at every image pixel. Padded cells are shown with a hatched pattern.
